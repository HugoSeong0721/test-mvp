Glance MPG — Privacy Policy

Effective October 3, 2026

Glance MPG ("the app") is a gas mileage and car maintenance log. The app has no accounts, the developer does not collect your personal information, and your log is never sent to the developer. The app shows Google AdMob banner ads, described under "Advertising" below.

What the app stores on your device

  • Your vehicles' names
  • Fill-ups you log: date, odometer, fuel amount, price, notes
  • Service and other expenses you log: date, type, odometer, cost, notes
  • Maintenance reminders you set, and your unit settings (miles/km, gallons/liters)

This information stays only on your iPhone. Like other app data, it is included in your iPhone's own iCloud or computer backup if you have that turned on. It is deleted when you delete the app. You can export all of it as a CSV file at any time (More → Export CSV) and delete any record or vehicle in the app.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, maintenance reminders are scheduled locally on your iPhone. No server is involved and nothing is sent anywhere.

Files you import or export

When you export, the app creates a CSV file and hands it to the iOS share sheet; you choose where it goes (Files, Mail, AirDrop…). When you import, the app reads only the file you pick.

Network use

All calculations (MPG, costs, reminder dates) happen on your device and the log works fully offline. The app connects to the internet only to load ads (below). It does not use your location.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app shows a banner ad provided by Google LLC through AdMob. To serve ads, Google may collect your device's advertising identifier (IDFA on iOS), IP address, and device and app information. This data is processed by Google, not by the developer. See the Google advertising policies and the Google Privacy Policy.

The app does not ask for tracking permission, so your advertising identifier is not shared and non-personalized ads are shown. No ads are shown on the screens where you enter a fill-up or a service record.

What the developer does not collect

  • Names, email addresses, phone numbers or other contact information
  • Your fill-ups, costs, odometer readings, vehicles or notes
  • Location
  • Analytics or usage statistics (the app contains no analytics SDK)

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13, and the developer does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
