This Privacy Policy explains how [DEVELOPER NAME] ("we", "us") handles information in connection with the mobile applications listed below. We built these apps to collect as little as possible. We do not ask you to create an account, and we do not sell your data.

2. Information we do not collect

Across all apps listed above, we do not collect, request, or store:

  • Your name, email address, or phone number
  • Account credentials — the apps have no sign-in
  • Your precise or approximate location

  • Health, financial, or payment card information

4. Advertising and your choices

Our free apps display ads served by Google AdMob. Ads may be personalized based on your Advertising ID. You are in control of this:

  • Reset or delete your Advertising ID — Android Settings → Privacy → Ads
  • Opt out of ad personalization — the same screen. You will still see ads, but they will be less relevant
  • Remove ads entirely — Som Currency offers a one-time in-app purchase that turns ads off

5. Children's privacy

These apps are not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

6. Data retention and deletion

Because we operate no user accounts and no server-side user database for these apps, we hold no personal data to retain or delete on your behalf.

  • Local data — removed when you uninstall the app, or by clearing the app's storage in Android settings
  • Third-party data — managed by Google under the policies linked in section 3.2. You can reset your Advertising ID at any time

To ask a question or make a request about your data, email us at [CONTACT EMAIL]. We aim to respond within 30 days.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or restrict processing of your personal information, and to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority. Because we do not collect identifying information, we usually cannot locate any data tied to you. Requests concerning data held by Google should be directed to Google.

We do not sell or share personal information as those terms are defined under the California Consumer Privacy Act.

8. Data security

App preferences remain in the app's private storage on your device. Network requests use encrypted HTTPS connections. No method of transmission is completely secure, but we keep the amount of data involved as small as possible — the strongest protection we can offer is simply not collecting the data.

9. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy we will update the "Last updated" date above. Material changes will be reflected in the app listing. Continuing to use the apps after an update means you accept the revised policy.
