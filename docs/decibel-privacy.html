Glance dB: Decibel Meter — Privacy Policy

Effective October 3, 2026

Glance dB ("the app") measures how loud it is around you. The app has no accounts, the developer does not collect your personal information, and no data is sent to the developer's servers. The app shows Google AdMob banner ads, described under "Advertising" below.

Microphone

The app uses your device's microphone only to measure sound level while the app is open on screen. The sound is turned into numbers (decibels) on your device as it comes in. Audio is never recorded, saved to a file, or sent anywhere. Measuring stops when you pause, leave the app, or lock the screen.

What the app stores on your device

  • Your past measurements: start time, duration, and the sound level numbers for each second (average and peak), plus any note you type on a report
  • Your settings: calibration adjustment, frequency weighting (dBA/dBC/dBZ), and whether to keep the screen on
  • Whether you have seen the welcome screen

This information stays only on your device. You can delete any measurement in History, and everything is deleted when you delete the app.

Sharing reports

When you tap "Save or Share Image", the app creates a picture of the report and opens the standard iOS share sheet. Where it goes next (Photos, Messages, Mail, etc.) is your choice; the developer never receives it.

Network use

Measuring works fully offline. The app connects to the internet only to load ads (below).

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app shows a banner ad provided by Google LLC through AdMob. The app never shows full-screen ads or ads with sound while you measure. To serve ads, Google may collect your device's advertising identifier (IDFA on iOS, Advertising ID on Android), IP address, and device and app information. This data is processed by Google, not by the developer. Google does not receive any microphone audio or sound measurements. See the Google advertising policies and the Google Privacy Policy.

On iOS the app does not ask for tracking permission, so your advertising identifier is not shared and non-personalized ads are shown. On Android you can turn off ads personalization in your device settings.

What the developer does not collect

  • Audio recordings of any kind
  • Names, email addresses, phone numbers or other contact information
  • Location
  • Analytics or usage statistics (the app contains no analytics SDK)

Accuracy

Phone microphones are not certified sound level meters. Readings are estimates for everyday use and are not intended for legal, medical or workplace compliance measurements.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13, and the developer does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
