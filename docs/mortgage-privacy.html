Glance Mortgage — Privacy Policy

Effective October 3, 2026

Glance Mortgage ("the app") is a mortgage and loan payment calculator. The app has no accounts, the developer does not collect your personal information, and no data is sent to the developer's servers. The app shows Google AdMob banner ads, described under "Advertising" below.

What the app stores on your device

  • The numbers you enter (price, down payment, interest rate, loan term, taxes and fees, extra payments) for each calculator, so they are still there next time
  • Which calculator you used last (mortgage, auto or personal loan)

This information stays only on your device and is deleted when you delete the app. "Reset to examples" in the app also clears it.

Network use

All calculations happen on your device, so the calculator works fully offline. The app connects to the internet only to load ads (below). It does not look up interest rates, credit scores or any other financial data about you.

Not financial advice

Results are estimates for planning only. The app is not a lender, does not offer credit, and does not ask for income, account or Social Security numbers.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app shows a banner ad provided by Google LLC through AdMob. To serve ads, Google may collect your device's advertising identifier (IDFA on iOS, Advertising ID on Android), IP address, and device and app information. This data is processed by Google, not by the developer. See the Google advertising policies and the Google Privacy Policy.

On iOS the app does not ask for tracking permission, so your advertising identifier is not shared and non-personalized ads are shown. On Android you can turn off ads personalization in your device settings.

What the developer does not collect

  • Names, email addresses, phone numbers or other contact information
  • The numbers you enter in the calculator
  • Location
  • Analytics or usage statistics (the app contains no analytics SDK)

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13, and the developer does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
