Catdoku: Cat Logic Puzzle — Privacy Policy

Effective October 2, 2026

Catdoku ("the app") is a cat logic puzzle game. The app has no accounts, the developer does not collect your personal information, and no data is sent to the developer's servers. The app shows Google AdMob ads, described under "Advertising" below.

What the app stores on your device

  • Your puzzle progress: the level you are on, the days you solved the daily puzzle and your times, and your streak
  • Whether you have seen the "How to play" screen

This information stays only on your device and is deleted when you delete the app.

Network use

Puzzles are generated on your device, so the game works fully offline. The app connects to the internet only to load ads (below).

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app shows a banner ad and, only when you choose to watch one, a rewarded video ad (for a hint or extra hearts), provided by Google LLC through AdMob. To serve ads, Google may collect your device's advertising identifier (IDFA on iOS, Advertising ID on Android), IP address, and device and app information. This data is processed by Google, not by the developer. See the Google advertising policies and the Google Privacy Policy.

On iOS the app does not ask for tracking permission, so your advertising identifier is not shared and non-personalized ads are shown. On Android you can turn off ads personalization in your device settings.

What the developer does not collect

  • Names, email addresses, phone numbers or other contact information
  • Location
  • Analytics or usage statistics (the app contains no analytics SDK)

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13, and the developer does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
