Solunar — Privacy Policy

Effective October 3, 2026

This fishing and hunting times app ("the app") works out solunar periods, sunrise, sunset, moon times and legal shooting light from the positions of the sun and moon. The app has no accounts, the developer does not collect your personal information, and no data is sent to the developer's servers. The app shows Google AdMob ads, described under "Advertising" below.

Location

If you tap "Use My Location", the app asks iOS for your location and uses it on your device to calculate times for where you are and to name the nearest town. Your location is not sent to the developer or anyone else. If you save a spot (for example, "Deer stand"), its coordinates are stored only on your device. You can say no and search for a town instead, and you can change the permission any time in iOS Settings.

What the app stores on your device

  • The place you chose and the places you saved
  • Your legal shooting light settings (minutes before sunrise and after sunset)
  • When the 30-day calendar was last opened by watching a video

This information stays only on your device and is deleted when you delete the app.

Network use

All times are calculated on your device; the app does not need the internet for them, and it does not send your location anywhere. The town list (from GeoNames, CC BY 4.0) is built into the app. The app connects to the internet only to load ads (below).

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app shows a banner ad, and an optional short video ad if you choose to watch one to open the 30-day calendar. Ads are provided by Google LLC through AdMob. To serve ads, Google may collect your device's advertising identifier (IDFA), IP address, and device and app information. This data is processed by Google, not by the developer. See the Google advertising policies and the Google Privacy Policy.

The app does not ask for tracking permission, so your advertising identifier is not shared and non-personalized ads are shown.

What the developer does not collect

  • Names, email addresses, phone numbers or other contact information
  • Your location or saved spots (they never leave your device)
  • Analytics or usage statistics (the app contains no analytics SDK)

A guide, not legal advice

Solunar times and scores are a guide based on sun and moon cycles, not a promise of fish or game. Shooting hours are set by each state and differ by species and season — always check your state's current hunting regulations.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13, and the developer does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
