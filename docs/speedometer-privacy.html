Glance Speed: GPS Speedometer — Privacy Policy

Soulfulfill · Effective October 3, 2026

This policy covers Glance Speed: GPS Speedometer for iPhone ("the app"), made by Soulfulfill. The app shows your speed, a speed alert you set yourself and your trip totals. It has no accounts, and the developer does not collect your personal information. The app shows Google AdMob banner ads, described under "Advertising" below.

Location

  • The app asks for your location only while the app is open on screen ("While Using the App"). It does not use location in the background.
  • Your location is used only on your iPhone to calculate speed, distance and trip time. It is never uploaded, shared, sold, or given to advertisers.
  • The app does not keep a history of where you have been. It keeps only trip totals (see below).

What the app stores on your device

  • Trip totals for each mode (car, bike, run, boat): top speed, average speed, distance and time
  • Your settings: mode, units, display style, speed alert, sound, HUD mirror, and whether you have seen the safety notice

This information stays only on your device and is deleted when you delete the app. "Reset trip" clears the trip totals at any time.

Network use

Speed is measured by your phone's GPS, so the speedometer works fully offline. The app connects to the internet only to load banner ads (below).

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app shows a banner ad at the bottom of the screen, provided by Google LLC through AdMob. It never shows full-screen or video ads. To serve ads, Google may collect your device's advertising identifier, IP address, and device and app information. This data is processed by Google, not by the developer, and the app does not pass your location to the ad service. See the Google advertising policies and the Google Privacy Policy.

The app does not ask for tracking permission on iOS, so your advertising identifier is not shared and non-personalized ads are shown.

What the developer does not collect

  • Your location or routes
  • Names, email addresses, phone numbers or other contact information
  • Analytics or usage statistics (the app contains no analytics SDK)

Safety

Do not interact with the app while driving. The app does not show road speed limits; the speed alert is a number you choose. Always follow posted limits.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13, and the developer does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
