Tides — Privacy Policy

Effective October 3, 2026

This tide app ("the app") shows NOAA tide predictions. The app has no accounts, the developer does not collect your personal information, and no data is sent to the developer's servers. The app shows Google AdMob banner ads, described under "Advertising" below.

Location

If you tap "Use My Location", the app asks iOS for your approximate location and uses it on your device to pick the nearest NOAA tide station and show distances. Your location is not stored and not sent to the developer, NOAA or anyone else. You can say no and search for a station instead, and you can change the permission any time in iOS Settings.

What the app stores on your device

  • The station you chose, your favorite stations and your height units (feet or meters)
  • The most recent NOAA predictions for stations you opened, so they show without signal

This information stays only on your device and is deleted when you delete the app.

Network use

The app downloads tide predictions from NOAA's public data service (api.tidesandcurrents.noaa.gov). Each request contains only the station ID, the dates and the units — not your location. NOAA's own privacy policy applies to its servers: noaa.gov/protecting-your-privacy. The app also connects to the internet to load ads (below). Sunrise, sunset and moon phase are calculated on your device.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app shows a banner ad provided by Google LLC through AdMob. There are no full-screen or video ads. To serve ads, Google may collect your device's advertising identifier (IDFA on iOS, Advertising ID on Android), IP address, and device and app information. This data is processed by Google, not by the developer. See the Google advertising policies and the Google Privacy Policy.

On iOS the app does not ask for tracking permission, so your advertising identifier is not shared and non-personalized ads are shown. On Android you can turn off ads personalization in your device settings.

What the developer does not collect

  • Names, email addresses, phone numbers or other contact information
  • Your location (it never leaves your device)
  • Analytics or usage statistics (the app contains no analytics SDK)

Not for navigation

Tide predictions are astronomical estimates and do not include wind, storms or river flow. Do not use the app for navigation.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13, and the developer does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
